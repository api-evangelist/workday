Two Workday Marketplace partner innovations show how human-centered AI works in practice: Cloverleaf embeds coaching into daily work flows to counter the reality that people forget about 70% of what they learn from traditional training, while Kainos redesigns compliance through contextual access reviews built into the Workday interface. Both illustrate AI that amplifies human reasoning, empathy, and leadership rather than removing human judgment from decisions.
