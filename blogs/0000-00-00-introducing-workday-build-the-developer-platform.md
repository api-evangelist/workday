Workday announced Workday Build, a developer platform for creating AI-powered solutions that includes a developer copilot trained on Workday's codebase, unified development tools, and integration with the Workday Flowise Agent Builder for visual AI agent creation. Customer examples include the PGA of America's purse management app and Target's 10-plus custom applications, and Workday is expanding its Built on Workday partner program and marketplace of over 100 partners amid 2x year-over-year developer community growth.
